# New in 2.1.1

- Restores 33 continuous days of St John response history, 15 September–17 October 2026, for all three priorities. The missing figures existed in the saved archive but had not reached the app. With permission, normalized public report figures are now included in the public feed and offline bundle; private Drive files and widget history remain private.
- Ramping: clearer hospital headings, a single Month in progress badge, explicit Ramping hours per day left axes, and a recent-month trend arrow comparing the current daily rate with the preceding three months combined. Detailed explanations and estimates are collapsed.
- T4 Wait: a taller chart, horizontal scrolling, fixed hour labels and date markers every 24 hours. It opens at the newest readings; swipe to see earlier history.
- Roster: new Week pattern and Response views. Compare captured T4 shift medians by weekday/weekend and colour, or whole-day response-within-target percentages by weekday/weekend and ambulance priority. Counts are visible. The source does not provide shift-level ambulance response times, so those are not inferred.

Install WAEDWidget-v2.1.1.apk over the existing app without uninstalling or clearing storage. Signing identity, widget and history storage are unchanged; the release is gated on the real 1.5 upgrade and history-preservation test.
